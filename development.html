This is the app as it is today. Scroll and it walks you through.

1

sanpo walks

Tell Sanpo how long you want to wander: short, medium or long. It builds a walking route of local stops around you, with a map, the distance and a time estimate.

Each stop shows what it is, how far away it is, its rating, and a "local's pick" badge for the places locals love. Turn on "include chains" if you want them, or step through the route one stop at a time.

3 min · 240 m

2

what's nearby

Hidden local gems just steps away, from quiet cafes and bookshops to shrines and sentos, based on where you're staying.

foodcafes & sweetssightsshoppingeverydaydrinks & nightlife

Pick a category, or search for something like ramen. You'll see top picks with a photo of the storefront, whether it's open now, how far it is and its rating, as a list or on a map.

2 min · 80 m

3

scenarios

In a pinch? Get the rundown on common situations, like using a convenience store or getting around on public transport.

Take Arriving in Japan: a step-by-step walkthrough, in order, from following the signs and your QR code to the booth, baggage, customs and arrivals.

6 min · 430 m

4

quick translations

Easy, common, kind and natural. Choose what you want to say, then show your phone to whoever you need to, whether that's a station worker or a shop assistant.

The Japanese appears upside down at the top of the screen so it faces the person across from you.

4 min · 310 m

5

saved places

Tap to save your favorite spots as you explore, whether it's a hidden soba shop or a peaceful sento. Build a collection to revisit later.

Found a place somewhere else? Add it straight from a link.
