Features

A look at what we've built so far. These are demos from the app in development.

01

What's Nearby

Discover hidden local gems just steps away, from quiet cafes and bookshops to shrines and sentos, based on where you're staying.

02

Saved Places

Tap to save your favorite spots as you explore, whether it's a hidden soba shop or a peaceful sento. Build your own collection of places to revisit later.

03

Scenarios

In a pinch? Get the rundown on common situations you'll find yourself in, whether it's using a convenience store or navigating the public transportation system.

04

Quick Translations

Easy, common, kind and natural translations. Simply show your device to whoever you need to, whether it's a station worker or a staff member.
