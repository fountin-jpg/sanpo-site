Features

A look at the app as it is today.

01

What's Nearby

Discover hidden local gems just steps away, from quiet cafes and bookshops to shrines and sentos, based on where you're staying.

fooddrinks & nightlifecafes & sweetssightsshoppingeveryday

02

Scenarios

In a pinch? Get the rundown on common situations you'll find yourself in, whether it's using a convenience store or navigating the public transportation system.

Take Arriving in Japan: a step-by-step walkthrough, in order, from following the signs and your QR code to the booth, baggage, customs and arrivals.

03

Quick Translations

Easy, common, kind and natural translations. Choose what you want to say, then simply show your device to whoever you need to, whether it's a station worker or a staff member.

The Japanese appears upside down at the top of the screen so it faces the person across from you.

04

Saved Places

Tap to save your favorite spots as you explore, whether it's a hidden soba shop or a peaceful sento. Build your own collection of places to revisit later.
