privacy.

Last updated: 3 November 2026.

Who we are. Sanpo is made by Popsiclestickairport LLC. Contact: TODO: add a contact email.

Your location. Sanpo asks for your location when you use something that needs it, like a walk or what's nearby. It sends your approximate surroundings to Google (Google Places) to find places near you. Sanpo does not keep a location history on a server. If you say no, walks and what's nearby won't work. The guides, phrase cards and conversations still do.

What goes to Google. Your coordinates and any words you search for are sent to Google's Places service to get results, photos, reviews and the review summary. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy. Where Google's summary is shown, it's labelled "Summarized with Gemini".

On your phone. Your saved places, routes, notes, photos you add to pins, settings, walk history and usage counters are stored on your phone only. Sanpo has no accounts and no server of its own that stores your data.

Camera and photos. The camera is used only when you choose to add a photo to a route pin. Photos you pick stay on your phone.

Tracking and ads. Sanpo does not track you, show ads or sell data. It includes no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children. Sanpo is not directed at children under 13.
